Mildly Elevated Liver Enzymes Template

For incidental asymptomatic AST/ALT elevation < 5x the upper limit of normal with no obvious acute illness.

  • AST/ALT < 5x the upper limit of normal
  • ALP not disproportionately elevated
  • Bilirubin normal or minimally elevated
  • No jaundice, confusion/encephalopathy, significant RUQ pain, or systemic toxicity

MASLD: Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease

MASH: Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis

If ALP predominantely elevated, use the cholestatic workup instead

Common Causes:

  • Alcohol
  • New medications (incl. acetaminophen, anabolic steroids)
  • supplements
    • valerian
    • kava
    • ashwagandha
    • green tea extract
    • turmeric
    • black cohosh
    • garcinia
    • kratom
    • red yeast rice extract
  • Viral hepatitis
  • Recent strenuous exercise
  • Metabolic causes: obesity, diabetes, dyslipidemia, hypertension
