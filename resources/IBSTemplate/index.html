### Treatment GENERAL: - regular meals; adequate hydration - trial low FODMAP diet x 4-6 weeks with food journaling, reintroduce foods gradually - regular moderate intensity exercise, 20-30 mins per day 3-5 times per week ABDOMINAL PAIN: - enteric coated peppermint oil (IBGard) - dicyclomine 10–20 mg PO QID PRN for cramping; watch for anticholinergic effects - (dry mouth, blurred vision, constipation, urinary retention, confusion) IBS-C: - psyllium fiber; start at 1 tsp daily and gradually titrate up (may cause bloating) - miralax, 17 g daily; helps with constipation, will not improve abdominal pain/bloating - linaclotide 290 mcg daily, expect improvement by day 7, stop if no improvement by 4 weeks - if diarrhea develops, reduce to 145 mcg daily, then 72 mcg daily if it persists IBS-D: - 2 mg PO PRN or ~30–45 min before meals/anticipated triggers; titrate to lowest effective dose, limit to 16 mg max per day - use the lowest dose needed to control symptoms - helps with diarrhea, does not improve abdominal pain - (for diarrhea + severe pain): nortriptyline 10 mg PO QHS; titrate up to effective dose - (for frequent diarrhea + little pain): rifaximin 550 mg PO TID x 14 days - consider retreatment if initially responsive, but symptoms recur
