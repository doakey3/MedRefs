Pulmonary Function Test Interpretation


Abbreviation Definition
FEV1 Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 second — the amount of air a person can forcefully exhale during the first second of a breath out.
FVC Forced Vital Capacity — the total amount of air a person can forcefully exhale after taking the deepest breath possible.
DLCO Diffusing Capacity of the Lung for Carbon Monoxide — measures how well gas moves from the lungs into the bloodstream.
TLC Total Lung Capacity — the total amount of air in the lungs after taking the deepest breath possible.
