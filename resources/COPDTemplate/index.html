How to Use COPD Medication Devices

Visual Analog Scale


  • COPD = a ratio of less than 0.7 for forced expiratory volume in one second (FEV1)/forced vital capacity (FVC) after bronchodilator use.
  • always confirm the diagnosis with spirometry.
  • Check for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency in patients diagnosed with COPD, especially if diagnosed prior to age 45
  • Inhaler technique matters. For those who struggle, consider nebulizer treatment.
