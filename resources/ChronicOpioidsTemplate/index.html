Opioid use disorder: Pharmacologic management

Every Visit Checklist

  • Confirm opioid, dose, frequency, indication, and calculate MME/day
  • Assess pain and functional benefit
  • Assess adverse effects, sedation, falls, overdose, and other opioid-related harms
  • Check PMP/PDMP at least every 3 months
  • Review other sedatives: benzodiazepines, alcohol, sleep medications, etc.
  • Screen for concerning use / opioid use disorder when indicated
  • offer naloxone
  • Consider urine/toxicology testing per risk and clinic policy
  • Optimize non-opioid and non-pharmacologic pain treatment
  • Decide: continue, taper, or transition/treat OUD
MME/day Interpretation
<20 Lower risk
20–49 Moderate risk
50–99 Higher risk, reassess carefully, emphasize naloxone safety
≥100 High risk, strong scrutiny of benefit vs harm
