Elevated Bilirubin

Direct bilirubin ≈ conjugated bilirubin
Indirect bilirubin ≈ unconjugated bilirubin

Medications That Cause ↑Indirect Bilirubin

  • Atazanavir (UGT1A1 inhibition)
  • Indinavir (UGT1A1 inhibition)
  • Rifampin (impaired hepatic uptake)
  • Probenecid (impaired hepatic uptake)
  • Gemfibrozil (impaired hepatic uptake)
  • Sorafenib (UGT1A1 inhibition)
  • Nilotinib (UGT1A1 inhibition)
  • Pazopanib (UGT1A1 inhibition)
  • Dapsone (hemolysis)
  • Primaquine (hemolysis, especially with G6PD deficiency)
  • Sulfonamides (hemolysis, especially with G6PD deficiency)
  • Nitrofurantoin (hemolysis, especially with G6PD deficiency)
  • Rasburicase (hemolysis with G6PD deficiency)
