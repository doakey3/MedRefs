Asthma Action Plan

Signs of Impending Respiratory Failure

  • Cyanosis
  • Confusion
  • Exhausted from breathing
  • SpO2 < 90%

Severe Exacerbation

  • Single-word statements
  • Tripoding or hunched-forward
  • Agitated, diaphoresis
  • Heart rate ≥ 120 bpm
  • Room air SpO2 < 90%
  • Peak Flow ≤ 50%
