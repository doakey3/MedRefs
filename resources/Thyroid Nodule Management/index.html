Thyroid Nodule Management


Sonographic Criteria for FNA

  • Irregular margins
  • Microcalcifications
  • Taller-than-wide shape
  • Extrathyroidal extension
  • Disrupted rim calcification
  • Cervical lymphadenopathy

Note: If extrathyroidal extension or cervical lymphadenopathy is present, perform FNA regardless of nodule size.
