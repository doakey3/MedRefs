Dyslipidemia

### Lifestyle Measures for ASCVD Prevention - Replace saturated fats with unsaturated fats - Minimize trans fats - Emphasize vegetables, fruits, whole grains, legumes, nuts, and seeds - Consume adequate soluble fiber (Dietary fiber ≥14 g/1000 calories; emphasize soluble/viscous fiber) - Achieve and maintain a healthy weight - 150 mins/week moderate-intensity aerobic exercise - Resistance training ≥ 2 d/wk - Avoid tobacco
### Lifestyle Measures for Triglycerides 150-499 - Minimize added sugars - Minimize saturated fat - Minimize alcohol - Achieve and maintain a healthy weight - 150 mins/week exercise - Resistance training ≥ 2 d/wk
### Lifestyle Measures for Triglycerides 500-999 - Minimize added sugars - Minimize saturated fat - Eliminate alcohol - Low fat diet (20-25% of calories) - Achieve and maintain a healthy weight - 150 mins/week exercise - Resistance training ≥ 2 d/wk
### Lifestyle Measures for Triglycerides ≥ 1000 - Eliminate added sugars - Minimize saturated fat - Eliminate alcohol - Low fat diet (10-15% of calories) - Achieve and maintain a healthy weight - 150 mins/week exercise - Resistance training ≥ 2 d/wk

Who Gets Lipid Panel Screening

  • Kids aged 9-11
  • Young adults 17-21 (AAP)
  • adults ≥19 at least q5y (ACC/AHA)
  • Adults: increase screening frequency with age and ASCVD risk factors

For those who are stable on treatment, check a lipid panel every 6-12 months

Fasting vs Non-Fasting

  • Non-fasting lipid panel is appropriate for most patients.
  • Get a fasting lipid panel if:
    • Family history of dyslipidemia or premature ASCVD (men < 55, women < 65)
    • Known or suspected triglyceride metabolism disorder
    • Non-fasting triglycerides ≥400 mg/dL
  • Consider fasting if non-fasting triglycerides ≥200 mg/dL and further evaluation of hypertriglyceridemia is needed.

Lipoprotein(a)

  • Check Lp(a) once in all adults, ideally with the first adult lipid panel.
  • Fasting is not required.
  • Usually does not need to be repeated.
  • Elevated: ≥125 nmol/L (≥50 mg/dL).
  • If high, be more aggressive about treatment.

Clinical ASCVD

  • Acute coronary syndrome (ACS)
  • History of myocardial infarction (MI)
  • Stable or unstable angina
  • Coronary or other arterial revascularization
  • Ischemic stroke
  • Transient ischemic attack (TIA)
  • Atherosclerotic peripheral arterial disease (PAD) including aortic aneurysm

Defining "Very High Risk"

  • ≥2 major ASCVD events
  • 1 major ASCVD event AND ≥ 2 high-risk conditions

Major ASCVD Events

  • ACS within the past 12 months
  • History of MI (other than recent ACS above)
  • History of ischemic stroke
  • Symptomatic PAD

High-Risk Conditions

  • Age ≥65 years
  • Prior CABG or PCI
  • Current smoking
  • Diabetes mellitus
  • History of heart failure
  • Hypertension
  • LDL-C ≥100 mg/dL despite maximally tolerated statin + ezetimibe

Cholesterol Management: Secondary Prevention for Adults at Very High Risk

  • Start the highest dose tolerated of a high-intensity statin
  • Goals:
    • ≥50% reduction LDL
    • LDL < 55
    • Non-HDL-C < 85
  • Recheck every 3 months until at goal
  • Add ezetimibe if needed
  • Add PCSK9 mAb if needed
  • If Unable to tolerate/obtain PCSK9 mAb, add Inclisiran
  • Add bempedoic acid if needed

Cholesterol Management: Secondary Prevention for Adults NOT at Very High Risk

  • Start the highest dose tolerated of a high-intensity statin
  • Goals:
    • ≥50% reduction LDL
    • LDL < 70
    • Non-HDL-C < 100
  • Recheck every 3 months until at goal
  • Add ezetimibe if needed
  • Add PCSK9 mAb if needed
  • Add bempedoic acid if needed
  • If Unable to tolerate/obtain PCSK9 mAb, add Inclisiran

ASCVD Risk Factors

  • Older age
  • Hypertension
  • Dyslipidemia
  • Cigarette smoking
  • Chronic kidney disease
  • Obesity

Severe Hypercholesterolemia (LDL-C ≥190 mg/dL)

  • Rule out/address secondary causes
  • Evaluate for heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HeFH)
  • Screen first-degree relatives
  • Start maximally tolerated statin

Diabetes-Specific ASCVD Risk Enhancers

  • Long duration of diabetes:
    • Type 2 diabetes: ≥10 years
    • Type 1 diabetes: ≥20 years
  • Albuminuria ≥30 µg/mg creatinine
  • eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m²
  • Retinopathy
  • Neuropathy
  • ABI <0.9

Other Primary Prevention Indications

  • CKD stage 3-4, age 40-75: Start moderate-intensity statin (with or without ezetimibe).
  • HIV, age 40-75: Start moderate-intensity statin.

ASCVD Elevated Risk

  • Established ASCVD
  • Diabetes mellitus plus ≥2 of the following additional ASCVD risk factors:
    • Age ≥50 years
    • Cigarette smoking
    • Hypertension
    • HDL-C ≤40 mg/dL for males or ≤50 mg/dL for females
    • hs-CRP >3 mg/L (0.3 mg/dL)
    • Creatinine clearance <60 mL/min
    • Retinopathy
    • Micro- or macroalbuminuria
    • ABI <0.9
