Mildly Elevated Liver Enzymes - Management

MASLD: Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease

MASH: Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis

Common Causes:

  • Alcohol
  • New medications (incl. acetaminophen, anabolic steroids)
  • supplements
    • valerian
    • kava
    • ashwagandha
    • green tea extract
    • turmeric
    • black cohosh
    • garcinia
    • kratom
    • red yeast rice extract
  • Viral hepatitis
  • Recent strenuous exercise
  • Metabolic causes: obesity, diabetes, dyslipidemia, hypertension

Workup Step 2

  • Hep A IGM
  • Heb B (SAg, SAb, CAbIGM)
  • Hep C Ab
  • ANA
  • Anti-Smooth Ab
  • Iron+Ferritin
  • Anti-Mitochondrial Ab if Alk Phos+GGT High
  • Cerulopslamin if age <50yo
  • Alpha-1 Antitrypsin
  • Serum IgG
  • Celiac Panel
  • TSH
